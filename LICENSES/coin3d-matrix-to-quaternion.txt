The complete SbRotation::setValue(const SbMatrix&) method was adapted on
2026-10-07 for the separately observed scalar memory convention. Adaptations
transpose the indexing signs, replace m33 by the observed literal one, capture
the actual writable index-table words, preserve fresh reads and store order,
skip the observed zero-root reciprocal and omit homogeneous scaling. This
does not assert unchanged method identity or ancestry of the original game.
